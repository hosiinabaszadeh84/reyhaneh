# Visual assets

Original `hero.webp` is the existing site's supplied image, extracted from its embedded data URL and optimized.

The following project assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, inspected, and optimized as WebP. UI text remains real, selectable Persian text.

| Asset | Production dimensions | Prompt / art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| envelope.webp | 900 × 628 | Blush cotton-paper envelope with burgundy heart wax seal, realistic editorial product photography, champagne rim light, transparent background, no text. One targeted edge-cleanup edit. |
| garden.webp | 1200 × 800 | Cinematic realistic secret garden at dusk, dark teal clear center, moss planting bed below, pink roses at side edges, ivory fireflies and warm upper-right light; no text. |
| rose.webp | 201 × 500 | Single realistic blush rose, slender green stem, two leaves, warm edge light, transparent, no pot/soil/text. |
| lantern.webp | 180 × 270 | Single antique champagne-brass lantern, clear glass and warm ivory light, realistic simple silhouette, transparent, no scenery/text. |
| glass-heart.webp | 560 × 560 | Symmetric front-view smoky blush glass heart with champagne highlights and faint internal pink light, optical depth, transparent, no stand/text. |
| compass-case.webp | 650 × 650 | Perfectly top-down brass compass case, thick polished rim and blank midnight-blue dial, subtle peripheral ticks, transparent exterior; no needle, lettering or symbols. Needle and labels use the site's existing native vector system. |

Vazirmatn: rastikerdar/vazirmatn, SIL OFL. Noto Naskh Arabic: google/fonts, SIL OFL. Lucide: installed distribution, ISC. Full license texts accompany the assets.

The invitation and chapter-two envelopes now use layered native CSS paper geometry for an actual hinged flap and sliding letter. The generated envelope WebP is retained in the repository as an optional visual asset, but is not requested by the page.
